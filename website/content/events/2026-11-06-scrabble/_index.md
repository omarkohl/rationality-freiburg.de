## Preparation

None, but it won't hurt to learn the [rules of the game](https://www.hasbro.com/common/instruct/Scrabble_(2003).pdf).
If you have a board, please bring it.

## What will we do?

We will shortly introduce the rules of the game, which are quite simple.
After that we'll explore how humans think about the game, when trying to find optimal moves, and compare
this to the approaches programmers took to teach computers to play the game.
We will see why this game is difficult for computers so that they are even today not considerably stronger than the top human players.

At the end, we will have some time for practice by playing the game ourselves.
