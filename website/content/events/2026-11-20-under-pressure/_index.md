## What will we do?

In theory, we all know how good decisions work: gather evidence, update beliefs
rationally, weigh options and act deliberately. But under time pressure,
incomplete information and acute overload, this model often breaks down in
practice. Instead, tunnel vision, paralyzing hierarchy barriers and seemingly
coordinated chaos take over.

In this meetup I will show you how decision-making and communication work in
high-risk environments. We will look at typical cognitive biases under acute
stress and work out together which tools and strategies from crisis management
can be put to concrete use to stay capable of acting under pressure in our own
(working) lives.
